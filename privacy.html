1. Who we are

The App is developed and operated by Goodwill Unachukwu Ogemdi, an independent developer ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, we are the controller of personal information processed through the App. You can contact us at focusflowapp.help@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to the FocusFlow mobile application for Android and to this website. It does not apply to third-party services that the App interacts with, such as Google, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Information we collect

The App can be used in two ways: without an account ("Guest mode") or signed in with a Google account. What we collect depends on which you choose.

3.1 Guest mode

In Guest mode, all information you enter into the App is stored only on your device. We do not receive, collect or have access to it.

3.2 When you sign in with Google

We never receive your Google password. Sign-in is handled by Google, which shares only the information listed above with us.

3.3 Purchases

Where in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Google Play. We receive a confirmation of the transaction (such as the item purchased and an order reference) so that we can deliver it to your account. We do not receive or store your payment card or other payment details.

3.4 Information that stays on your device

  • Reminders and alarms are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Alarm sound. If you select a ringtone or audio file as your alarm sound, the App stores a reference to it on your device and plays it locally. The file is never uploaded.
  • Home-screen widget content is generated on your device.
  • Error reports. If the App encounters an error, it may display the error and offer a "Send error" option. This opens your device's share menu; nothing is transmitted unless you choose to share it, and only to the recipient you select.

4. Information we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers or advertising networks.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, messages or call logs.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

5. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the App and its features;
  • authenticate you and secure your account;
  • back up your data and synchronise it across your devices;
  • deliver purchases made through Google Play;
  • respond to your requests, questions and support messages;
  • detect, prevent and address technical issues, fraud and abuse; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the App and the account features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the App secure, prevent abuse and improve reliability, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: for device permissions such as notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required by law.

8. How information is shared

We share personal information only as described below:

  • Service providers. We use Supabase to host our database and authentication, and Google for sign-in and payment processing. These providers process information on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers. If the App is transferred, merged or sold, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may store and process information on servers located outside your country of residence. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards offered by those providers, such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Data retention

  • Account information and backed-up data are retained for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your account and all backed-up data are permanently deleted from our database immediately. Residual copies in provider backups are removed in the normal course of those providers' backup cycles.
  • Data stored only on your device remains there until you erase it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Records of purchases may be retained by Google Play as required by applicable law.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and database access rules that allow each account to access only its own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and obtain a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Most of your information can be viewed, edited and deleted directly in the App. To exercise any other right, email focusflowapp.help@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

California residents. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

13. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete account), or by request by email. Full instructions are available on our Account Deletion page.

14. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or a higher minimum age where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

15. Third-party services and links

The App and this website may link to third-party services, including Google and Supabase. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

16. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where the changes are material, notify you within the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
