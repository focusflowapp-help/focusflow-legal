FocusFlow

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

FocusFlow is a planner app for Android, made by Goodwill Unachukwu Ogemdi ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email focusflowapp.help@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can use FocusFlow without an account. Then everything stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google, your plan is backed up so it's the same on all your phones.
  • No ads. No tracking. Your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

What's collected

If you use the app without signing in, your tasks, settings, streak, gems and history are stored only on your phone. Nothing is sent to me.

If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and a Google account ID from Google, used only to create your account and keep you signed in. It never gets your Google password.

Your plan. When you're signed in, the app backs up what you put in it: your tasks and to-dos (titles, notes, times, steps, categories, what you ticked), your settings, your streak, gems and the themes you own. This is what lets your plan show up on another phone.

Purchases (when gems can be bought). Payments are handled by Google Play. I receive a confirmation of what was bought, but never your card or payment details.

What stays on your phone

  • Reminders and alarms are set on your phone. The alarm sound you pick (a tone or a song) is only used by your phone; the file is never uploaded.
  • The home-screen widget reads your plan from your phone.
  • If the app crashes, it can show the error and offer a "Send error" button. That opens your phone's share menu, so nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and only to whoever you choose.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, to remind you about what you planned.
  • Alarms & reminders and full-screen alerts, so alarms ring on time and can show over the lock screen.
  • Audio files (only if you choose a song as your alarm), to play it.

Who else handles your data

  • Google, for sign-in and (later) Google Play payments. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Supabase, the cloud database that stores backups for signed-in users. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only read its own data.

How long it's kept

Your backup is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, your backup and account are deleted right away. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you erase it in the app or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • See or change your data: everything is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. You can also email me. See Delete your account.
  • Stop syncing: sign out. Your plan stays on that phone only.
  • Get a copy, or ask anything about your data: email focusflowapp.help@gmail.com.

Children

FocusFlow isn't made for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has signed in, email me and I'll delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app.
