Delete your FocusFlow account

This deletes your account and all the data backed up with it.

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open FocusFlow and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and backup are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email focusflowapp.help@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my FocusFlow account". It'll be deleted within 7 days and you'll get a reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email, Google account ID).
  • Your backed-up plan: tasks, to-dos, settings, streak, gems, themes and history.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Gems and themes can't be restored once the account is deleted. Records of purchases may be kept by Google Play as required by law.

If you never signed in, there's nothing stored with me. To remove the data on your phone, use Erase this device's data in Settings, or uninstall the app.
