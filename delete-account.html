Account and Data Deletion

FocusFlow  ·  Developer: Goodwill Unachukwu Ogemdi  ·  Last updated: November 24, 2026

You can request deletion of your FocusFlow account and all associated data at any time, using either of the methods below. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. Delete from within the App

  1. Open FocusFlow and sign in, if you are not already signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Your account and all backed-up data are deleted immediately.

2. Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the App, send an email to focusflowapp.help@gmail.com:

  • from the email address linked to your FocusFlow (Google) account;
  • with the subject line "Account deletion request".

We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 days, and confirm by email once deletion is complete.

3. What is deleted

DataAction
Account information (name, email address, Google account identifier)Permanently deleted
Planner content (tasks, to-dos, notes, steps, categories, completion records)Permanently deleted
App progress (streaks, daily results, gems, owned themes)Permanently deleted
App settingsPermanently deleted

4. What may be retained

  • Residual copies in our database provider's backups, which are removed in the normal course of its backup cycle.
  • Records of purchases held by Google Play, where required by law.

Gems, themes and other in-app items cannot be restored after deletion.

5. If you never signed in

If you used FocusFlow without an account, we hold no data about you. To remove the data stored on your device, select Settings → Erase this device's data in the App, or uninstall the App.

6. Contact

Questions about deletion: focusflowapp.help@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
