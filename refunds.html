1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for purchases made in the FocusFlow app (the "App"), developed by Goodwill Unachukwu Ogemdi ("we", "us"). It forms part of our Terms of Service. All purchases in the App are processed by Google Play.

2. What can be purchased

Where available, you may purchase gems, a virtual currency used inside the App to unlock items such as themes and streak flames ("Virtual Items"). Virtual Items have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash. Gems earned through use of the App are free and are not eligible for refunds.

3. Refunds through Google Play

Because payments are processed by Google Play, most refund requests are handled by Google:

WhenHow to request a refund
Within 48 hours of purchaseRequest a refund directly from Google Play: open Google Play order history, select the purchase and choose "Request a refund", or follow Google's refund instructions.
After 48 hoursContact us at focusflowapp.help@gmail.com. We review requests in line with this Policy and Google Play's policies.

Google may decide refund requests made through Google Play under its own policies, which we do not control.

4. When we will issue a refund

When you contact us, we will issue a refund or appropriate remedy where:

  • you were charged but the purchased gems were not delivered to your account and we cannot deliver them;
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase in error; or
  • a refund is required under applicable consumer protection law.

Where a purchase was not delivered, we may first try to deliver it to your account instead of issuing a refund.

5. When refunds are not available

Except where required by law, refunds are generally not available for:

  • gems that have already been spent on Virtual Items;
  • a change of mind after the gems have been delivered and used;
  • Virtual Items lost because you deleted your account, erased your device's data or uninstalled the App while in Guest mode; or
  • accounts suspended or terminated for breach of our Terms of Service.

6. Effect of a refund

If a purchase is refunded, the gems from that purchase will be removed from your account. If those gems have already been spent, the Virtual Items unlocked with them may be removed, or your gem balance may become negative until it is restored by future earnings.

7. How to contact us

Email focusflowapp.help@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund request" and include:

  • the email address of the Google account you signed in with;
  • your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA."), found in your purchase receipt email or Google Play order history;
  • the date of purchase; and
  • a short description of the problem.

We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Google Play to your original payment method; the time it takes to appear depends on Google and your payment provider.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this Policy affects any rights you may have under the consumer protection laws of the country in which you live.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Changes do not affect purchases made before the change, except where required by law.
